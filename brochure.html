What you get

Why choose WP Memberships?

Everything you need to run a thriving membership community in one unified WordPress dashboard—without juggling external forms, spreadsheets, or bloated add-ons.

✅

Applications and approvals

Visitors apply online. You approve or reject in one click, or add members yourself directly from the dashboard.

🏢

Membership levels

Individual and Corporate levels built right in. Suspend or reinstate any member at any time with zero hassle.

👤

Member portal

A personal welcome, a dedicated menu bar, and an easy-to-use details form for every approved member.

🖼️

Profile photos

Photos are resized automatically for crisp, fast-loading display in the member directory and on each profile.

✍️

Easy writing tool

Members write with a friendly Markdown toolbar and see a live preview as they type. No code needed.

📰

Posts that go live

Approved member posts are published automatically as regular blog posts for everyone on your site to read.

How it works

Five simple steps from install to community

Get your membership hub running in minutes with automated page setup and clear role visibility.

1

Install

Upload the plugin and click Activate. Three essential pages are created automatically.

2

Recruit

Visitors apply through the Join page, or you add members directly from your admin panel.

3

Approve

Review applicants in one unified list and decide with a single click.

4

Engage

Members complete their profiles, upload a photo, and share posts using the built-in editor.

5

Publish

You approve member content, and it appears immediately as a regular post on your blog.

Access Control

Who sees what

Logged-out visitors

See the Join page, the public part of the directory, and published member posts.

Approved members

See the full directory, photos and bios, the member portal, and the Markdown editor.

Administrators

Manage everything—applicants, levels, suspensions, and post approvals—from the Memberships page.

Architecture & Privacy

Built to last

Engineered cleanly on native WordPress standards for security, speed, and complete independence.

🗄️

Your data, your site

Everything is stored on your own WordPress site. No third-party accounts, external APIs, or recurring SaaS lock-in required.

🧹

Clean removal

Deleting the plugin cleanly removes its settings and plugin data, while approved member blog posts stay safely on your site.

🛡️

Secure by design

Every form submission and action is protected with native WordPress nonce security checks and strict content sanitization.

🌐

Free and open source

Released under GPL-2.0-or-later, with complete, auditable source code available directly on GitHub.

Technical requirements

What your server needs

  • ✓ WordPress 6.0 or newer
  • ✓ PHP 8.1 or newer
  • ✓ GD or Imagick PHP image library (for automatic profile photo resizing)
  • ✓ Standard WordPress hosting—no external accounts or paid add-ons needed
