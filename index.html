FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. The full guide is in the help documentation.

Does it send emails?

No. You share login details with new members yourself, so there is no email setup to manage.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything is managed from the Memberships page in your dashboard.

Can members edit their posts after approval?

No. They delete the post and submit a new version for approval.

What do I need on my hosting?

WordPress 6.0 or newer, PHP 8.1 or newer, and the GD image library (enabled on most hosts).

Is it free?

Yes. It is released under the GPL-2.0-or-later license.
